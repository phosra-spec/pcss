# §1 Charter

The specification itself, governance-stamped.

Charter is the meta-document — it is what you are reading. Charter governs the spec's existence, its versioning, its conformance criteria, and its license.

For the full text, see [`v1/pcss-v1.0.md`](../pcss-v1.0.md).

## What Charter is

- A canonical, versioned, signed document.
- Public-review status (not yet ratified by an independent body).
- Licensed CC BY 4.0.
- Stewarded by the Phosra Standards Body during public review (see [GOVERNANCE.md](../../GOVERNANCE.md)).

## What Charter is not

- A piece of executable code. The other eight capabilities have wire formats; Charter is documentation.
- A trademark license. The "Phosra Conformant" badge is governed separately; see [Trademark policy on phosra.com](https://www.phosra.com/legal/trademark).
- An endorsement of any particular implementer.
